The "God File" Architecture: Why It Feels Complicated

Right now, almost the entire customer application is tightly bundled into a single file (Storefront.jsx at 2,961 lines), and almost all admin features are bundled into AdminPanel.jsx (1,429 lines). Here is the actual visual representation of how they are running:
